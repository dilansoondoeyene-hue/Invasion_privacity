Invasión is a free 3D shooter game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses.
1. Information stored on your device
The game saves your progress (levels, coins, unlocked items, settings and your chosen player name) only on your device. We do not receive this data. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, it is deleted.
2. Online multiplayer
If you use online rooms, the game connects to a relay server that passes messages between the players in the same room. The messages include your player name, your position and actions in the match, and chat messages. The server does not save this information; it only forwards it while the room is open. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and connection times, for security and operation.
Chat messages are visible to the other players in your room. Do not share personal information in chat. You can mute any player by tapping their message.
3. Challenges to the administrator
If you send a challenge to the administrator, we store your chosen player name, the time of the challenge and a random identifier of your device on a server (Cloudflare) for up to 7 days or until the challenge is accepted or cancelled.
4. Purchases and codes
The optional "Pack" is sold outside the game through Ko-fi. Payments are handled by Ko-fi and its payment partners; we never receive your card or payment details. To unlock the pack, we store the email address of the buyer provided by Ko-fi and the code generated for you, together with a random identifier of the device that redeems the code, on a server (Cloudflare). We use this only to deliver and verify your purchase. Gift codes are linked in the same way to the device that redeems them.
5. What we do not do
The game does not show ads.
The game does not use analytics or advertising trackers.
We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except the service providers needed to run the game (Render for the room server, Cloudflare for codes and challenges, and Ko-fi for payments).
The game does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.
6. Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
7. Your rights and deletion
You can ask us to delete the data we hold about you (for example, your purchase email or code record) by writing to [YOUR EMAIL]. Please include the email used for the purchase or the room/player name.
8. Third-party content
